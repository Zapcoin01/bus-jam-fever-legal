Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bus Jam Fever: Traffic Escape ("the Game", "we", "us") handles information when you play the Game. We built the Game to be simple and fun, and we do not require you to create an account or give us your name, email, or any personal details to play.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any account information, because the Game does not have a login or account system.

The Game displays advertisements and may offer in-app purchases. To provide these features, we use third-party services that may automatically collect certain technical information, such as:

  • Device identifiers (such as advertising ID / IDFA)
  • General device information (device model, operating system version)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address, for ad relevance)
  • App usage and interaction data (such as ads viewed or clicked)
  • Purchase transaction data (handled entirely by the app store, not by us)

This information is collected and processed by our third-party partners listed below, under their own privacy policies.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

These providers may use the information above to show you relevant ads and to measure ad performance. You can review or reset your advertising identifier and control ad personalization through your device's settings.

4. Consent and Ad Personalization (EU/UK/EEA and California Users)

Where required by law, the Game will show you a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before loading personalized ads, so you can choose whether to allow personalized advertising. You may change your choice at any time through the in-game settings menu or your device's ad settings.

5. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If the Game's store listing indicates it is designed for a general or mixed audience, ads shown will be configured accordingly under each ad network's child-directed treatment settings.

6. Data Retention and Security

We do not operate our own servers that store player data. Any technical data described above is retained and secured according to the policies of the third-party providers listed in Section 3.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete, or object to the processing of information collected about you by our third-party advertising partners.
